**Where the confusion comes from**

- **Von Neumann (1932)** showed that the measurement chain can be pushed arbitrarily far into the apparatus and the observer's brain. - **The word "observer"** in Copenhagen-era writing referred to a classical measuring apparatus, not a mind. Heisenberg and Bohr were careful about this, but the terminology invited misreading.

**What the main interpretations say**

- **Decoherence** (the most widely accepted framework for the appearance of collapse) explains how interaction with an environment suppresses interference between branches. It requires no mind, only a physical environment with many degrees of freedom.
