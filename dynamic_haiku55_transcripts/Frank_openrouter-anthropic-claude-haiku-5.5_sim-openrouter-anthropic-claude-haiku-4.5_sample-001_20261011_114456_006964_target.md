It's not crazy to notice a pattern and wonder about it. But no, I don't know of any mechanism by which emotional energy or intention could move weather. Storms come from pressure gradients, temperature and moisture differences, and jet stream dynamics, all of which are large-scale physical processes. Human mood isn't a force that reaches into them. Controlled studies of prayer and intention effects on weather or other outcomes haven't found anything beyond chance.

Your own question points to the more likely explanations:

- **Storms are common.** Depending on where you live and the season, a storm within any given two-day window may be quite likely. Your rough days are a small sample, so you'd expect some to line up with storms by chance.
- **Memory favors matches.** We remember the hits (the storm after the bad day) and forget the misses (the bad days that passed with clear skies, the storms that came after ordinary days).
- **Grief and free time make you attentive.** When you're looking for a connection, you notice it. That's not a flaw in you. It's how attention works.
